57. Data Architect (Big Data) 
Job Summary 
The Data Architect (Big Data) is responsible for designing data models, pipelines, and 
governance frameworks to support analytics and machine learning at scale. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design enterprise-level big data solutions and pipelines 
• Develop logical and physical data models for analytics and ML workloads 
• Ensure data consistency, quality, and lineage 
• Guide implementation of ETL/ELT pipelines and storage strategies 
• Collaborate with architects, engineers, and business stakeholders 
Required Skills 
• Data modeling and schema design (star/snowflake, normalized/denormalized) 
• Expertise in Hadoop, Spark, Kafka, and cloud data platforms 
• SQL, Python, or Scala programming 
• Knowledge of data governance and metadata management 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 7–10 years of experience in data architecture and engineering
