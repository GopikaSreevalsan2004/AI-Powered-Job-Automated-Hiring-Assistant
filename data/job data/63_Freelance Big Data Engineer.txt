63. Freelance Big Data Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Freelance Big Data Engineer works independently or with multiple clients to design, 
build, and maintain big data pipelines and platforms on a contract basis. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Develop scalable data pipelines using Hadoop, Spark, Kafka, or cloud services 
• Integrate data from multiple sources (structured, semi-structured, unstructured) 
• Ensure data quality, consistency, and performance optimization 
• Deliver solutions according to client requirements and deadlines 
• Provide documentation and knowledge transfer for deployed systems 
Required Skills 
• Strong programming in Python, Java, or Scala 
• Expertise in distributed computing frameworks (Hadoop, Spark) 
• SQL, data modeling, and cloud data platform experience 
• Self-management, communication, and client-facing skills 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or related field 
• 3–8 years of experience in big data engineering 
• Proven freelance or independent project experience preferred
