️. Principal Big Data Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Principal Big Data Engineer is a senior technical authority responsible for defining long
term data architecture, solving highly complex engineering problems, and guiding enterprise
wide data initiatives. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Define enterprise big data architecture and strategy 
• Design highly scalable, fault-tolerant data platforms 
• Evaluate and select big data technologies and frameworks 
• Solve complex performance, scalability, and reliability issues 
• Provide technical leadership across multiple teams 
• Influence data governance, security, and compliance policies 
• Partner with senior leadership on data-driven initiatives 
Required Skills 
• Expert-level knowledge of distributed systems 
• Deep understanding of cloud-native big data platforms 
• Proven experience designing systems at massive scale 
• Strong strategic thinking and technical leadership 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree (PhD preferred) 
• 10+ years of experience in Big Data and distributed systems
