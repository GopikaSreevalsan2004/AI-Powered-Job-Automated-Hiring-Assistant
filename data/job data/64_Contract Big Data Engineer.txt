64. Contract Big Data Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Contract Big Data Engineer is engaged on a temporary basis to support enterprise or 
startup data initiatives, delivering high-quality engineering solutions. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Implement and optimize big data pipelines and ETL/ELT workflows 
• Support ongoing data platform projects or migration initiatives 
• Ensure system reliability, scalability, and security 
• Collaborate with internal teams and stakeholders on requirements 
• Provide operational support and troubleshooting for contracted period 
Required Skills 
• Expertise in Hadoop, Spark, Kafka, or other distributed systems 
• SQL and programming skills (Python, Java, Scala) 
• Cloud platform knowledge (AWS, GCP, Azure) 
• Ability to quickly understand client systems and deliver solutions 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s degree 
• 3–7 years of big data engineering experience 
• Prior contract or consultancy experience is a plus
