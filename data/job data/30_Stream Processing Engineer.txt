30. Stream Processing Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Stream Processing Engineer builds, optimizes, and maintains data pipelines that process 
real-time streaming data for analytics, monitoring, and decision-making. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and implement real-time stream processing workflows 
• Optimize data throughput and latency 
• Ensure fault tolerance and reliability of streaming systems 
• Collaborate with stakeholders for real-time data requirements 
• Monitor and maintain processing infrastructure 
Required Skills 
• Experience with Spark Streaming, Flink, or Storm 
• Programming in Java, Scala, or Python 
• Knowledge of Kafka, cloud streaming services, and distributed systems 
• SQL and data transformation expertise 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or related field 
• 3–6 years of stream processing experience
