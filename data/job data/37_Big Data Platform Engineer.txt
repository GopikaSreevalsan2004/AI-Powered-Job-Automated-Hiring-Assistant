37. Big Data Platform Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Big Data Platform Engineer builds, maintains, and optimizes enterprise-scale big data 
platforms for analytics, AI, and operational workloads. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Architect and deploy scalable big data platforms (on-premise, cloud, or hybrid) 
• Ensure high availability, fault tolerance, and performance 
• Maintain data ingestion, processing, and storage pipelines 
• Implement monitoring, alerting, and automation for platform operations 
• Collaborate with data engineers, analysts, and DevOps teams 
Required Skills 
• Deep knowledge of Hadoop, Spark, Kafka, and cloud platforms 
• Strong programming and system design skills 
• Experience with data governance, security, and compliance 
• Familiarity with platform automation and DevOps tools 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 6–10 years of experience in big data platform engineering
