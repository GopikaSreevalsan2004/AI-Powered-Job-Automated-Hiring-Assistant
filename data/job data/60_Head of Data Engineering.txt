60. Head of Data Engineering 
Job Summary 
The Head of Data Engineering oversees all data engineering teams and initiatives, ensuring 
high-performance, scalable, and reliable data systems aligned with business objectives. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Lead multiple data engineering teams and projects 
• Define strategy for data platform, pipelines, and analytics support 
• Ensure adoption of best practices for engineering, security, and governance 
• Manage budgets, resources, and roadmap for data initiatives 
• Collaborate with C-level executives to align data strategy with business goals 
Required Skills 
• Leadership and team management experience 
• Expertise in big data, cloud platforms, and modern data engineering 
• Strategic planning, budgeting, and project management 
• Excellent communication and stakeholder management 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 10–15 years of experience in data engineering and leadership
