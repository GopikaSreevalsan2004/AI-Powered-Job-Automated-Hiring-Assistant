16. Senior Data Engineer (Big Data) 
Job Summary 
The Senior Data Engineer (Big Data) leads the design and implementation of complex data 
systems, mentors engineers, and ensures scalable and secure data platforms. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Architect large-scale data processing systems 
• Optimize performance of data pipelines and storage 
• Lead complex data integration initiatives 
• Review code and enforce engineering standards 
• Mentor junior engineers 
• Collaborate with stakeholders on data requirements 
Required Skills 
• Advanced Spark optimization and tuning 
• Cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP) 
• Data lake and warehouse architecture 
• Strong problem-solving skills 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 6–9 years of big data engineering experience
