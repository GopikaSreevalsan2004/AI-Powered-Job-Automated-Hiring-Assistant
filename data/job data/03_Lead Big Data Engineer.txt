3
️. Lead Big Data Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Lead Big Data Engineer is responsible for leading a team of engineers, owning end-to
end big data solutions, and aligning data engineering efforts with business objectives. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Lead and manage a team of Big Data Engineers 
• Architect and deliver scalable data platforms 
• Define technical standards, frameworks, and best practices 
• Oversee project delivery and technical execution 
• Act as the primary technical point of contact for stakeholders 
• Ensure system scalability, reliability, and cost efficiency 
• Drive adoption of new big data technologies 
Required Skills 
• Strong leadership and team management skills 
• Expertise in Hadoop, Spark, Kafka, and cloud data services 
• Experience with data platform modernization 
• Excellent communication and stakeholder management 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Engineering or Computer Science 
• 7–10 years of experience in data engineering, including leadership roles
