11. Apache Spark Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Apache Spark Engineer designs and manages enterprise-grade Spark-based data 
processing platforms, focusing on scalability, reliability, and cost efficiency. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Architect and maintain Spark-based data platforms 
• Optimize memory management and job execution 
• Implement real-time and batch data pipelines 
• Monitor Spark clusters and workloads 
• Ensure fault tolerance and system reliability 
• Guide best practices for Spark development 
Required Skills 
• Expert knowledge of Apache Spark and distributed computing 
• Experience with cloud-based Spark services (Databricks, EMR) 
• Strong performance tuning and debugging skills 
• Programming expertise in Scala or Python 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 5–8 years of Spark and big data experience
