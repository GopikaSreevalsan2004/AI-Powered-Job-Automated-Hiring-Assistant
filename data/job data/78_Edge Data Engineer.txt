78. Edge Data Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Edge Data Engineer designs data pipelines and analytics systems for edge devices, 
enabling real-time processing close to data sources. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Implement data collection, processing, and storage at edge locations 
• Optimize data pipelines for bandwidth, latency, and resource constraints 
• Ensure security, privacy, and reliability of edge data systems 
• Integrate edge data with centralized cloud or lakehouse platforms 
• Support analytics, AI, and ML applications on edge data 
Required Skills 
• Experience with IoT, edge computing, or distributed processing 
• Programming in Python, Java, or C++ 
• Knowledge of streaming frameworks and edge-to-cloud integration 
• Familiarity with security, compression, and low-latency optimization 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 4–7 years of experience in edge computing or real-time data systems
