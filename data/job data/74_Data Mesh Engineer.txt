74. Data Mesh Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Data Mesh Engineer implements decentralized, domain-oriented data architectures to 
enable scalable and self-serve data access across large organizations. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and implement data mesh architecture following domain-driven principles 
• Build self-service data pipelines and APIs for data consumers 
• Establish governance, data quality, and interoperability standards 
• Enable discoverability and cataloging of datasets across domains 
• Collaborate with product, analytics, and engineering teams 
Required Skills 
• Experience in data mesh concepts and architectures 
• Strong SQL, Python, or Scala programming skills 
• Familiarity with cloud-native data platforms and streaming systems 
• Knowledge of governance, cataloging, and metadata management 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 4–8 years of experience in distributed data architectures
