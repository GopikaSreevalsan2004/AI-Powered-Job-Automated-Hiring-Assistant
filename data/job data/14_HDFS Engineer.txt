14. HDFS Engineer 
Job Summary 
The HDFS Engineer manages and optimizes Hadoop Distributed File System (HDFS) to 
ensure reliable, secure, and high-performance data storage. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and manage HDFS storage architecture 
• Monitor and optimize data replication and availability 
• Troubleshoot storage and node failures 
• Implement backup, recovery, and security controls 
• Ensure high availability of NameNode services 
• Collaborate with Hadoop and infrastructure teams 
Required Skills 
• Deep expertise in HDFS internals 
• Strong Linux and system administration skills 
• Experience with cluster monitoring tools 
• Knowledge of Hadoop security and access controls 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering or related field 
• 4–7 years of HDFS and Hadoop storage experience
