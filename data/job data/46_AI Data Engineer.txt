46. AI Data Engineer 
Job Summary 
The AI Data Engineer designs, builds, and maintains data infrastructure for AI applications, 
ensuring high-quality, scalable datasets for training and inference. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Build and maintain AI data pipelines for structured and unstructured data 
• Collaborate with AI teams to define data requirements 
• Integrate multiple data sources and ensure consistency 
• Optimize storage and retrieval of large-scale AI datasets 
• Implement monitoring, versioning, and lineage for AI data 
Required Skills 
• Programming in Python or Scala 
• Knowledge of big data frameworks (Spark, Hadoop) 
• Experience with cloud AI platforms (AWS Sagemaker, Azure ML, GCP AI Platform) 
• Understanding of ML lifecycle and data preparation 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 3–7 years of experience in AI-focused data engineering
