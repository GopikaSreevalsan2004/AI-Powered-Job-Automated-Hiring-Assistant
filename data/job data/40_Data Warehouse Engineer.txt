40. Data Warehouse Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Data Warehouse Engineer designs, develops, and maintains enterprise data warehouses 
to support reporting, analytics, and business intelligence. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and implement data warehouse architectures 
• Develop ETL/ELT workflows to populate warehouse tables 
• Optimize queries and storage for performance 
• Monitor data quality and integrity 
• Collaborate with BI, analytics, and product teams 
• Document data models, pipelines, and schemas 
Required Skills 
• Strong SQL and data modeling skills 
• Experience with ETL/ELT tools 
• Knowledge of data warehousing concepts (star/snowflake schemas) 
• Familiarity with cloud or on-premise warehouse technologies 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s degree 
• 3–6 years of experience in data warehousing
