71. Trainee Big Data Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Trainee Big Data Engineer participates in hands-on training and real-world projects to 
build foundational skills in big data engineering and analytics pipelines. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Learn to design and implement data ingestion and processing pipelines 
• Assist in developing ETL workflows and data transformation scripts 
• Gain experience with cloud platforms, Spark, Hadoop, and Kafka 
• Conduct data quality checks and support debugging 
• Work under the supervision of senior engineers or mentors 
Required Skills 
• Basic programming skills (Python, Java, or Scala) 
• Understanding of SQL and data modeling basics 
• Interest in cloud technologies, analytics, and big data 
• Good learning attitude and teamwork skills 
Qualifications 
• Recent graduate or final-year student in Computer Science, Data Science, or related 
field 
• Academic projects or certifications in data engineering are a plus
