43. BigQuery Engineer 
Job Summary 
The BigQuery Engineer develops, manages, and optimizes Google BigQuery data 
warehouses and pipelines for large-scale analytics. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Build and maintain BigQuery datasets and ETL/ELT pipelines 
• Optimize query performance and storage costs 
• Implement data governance, security, and lineage 
• Integrate BigQuery with BI tools like Looker, Tableau, or Data Studio 
• Collaborate with analytics and data science teams 
Required Skills 
• Expert in Google BigQuery and SQL 
• Experience with Python or ETL/ELT tools 
• Knowledge of cloud architecture and data pipelines 
• Familiarity with large-scale analytics workflows 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s degree 
• 3–6 years of experience with BigQuery and cloud data engineering
