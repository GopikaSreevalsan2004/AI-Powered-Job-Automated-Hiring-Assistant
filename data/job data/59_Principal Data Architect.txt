59. Principal Data Architect 
Job Summary 
The Principal Data Architect leads the design of complex data solutions, mentors teams, and 
drives innovation in data architecture practices. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Lead architecture for enterprise data platforms and pipelines 
• Evaluate emerging technologies and recommend adoption strategies 
• Ensure scalability, performance, and reliability of data systems 
• Mentor architects and senior engineers on design and best practices 
• Collaborate with business and technical leadership on strategy 
Required Skills 
• Expert in distributed systems, cloud platforms, and big data technologies 
• Advanced data modeling and system design expertise 
• Strong leadership, mentoring, and strategic planning skills 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 12+ years of experience in data architecture and enterprise data systems
