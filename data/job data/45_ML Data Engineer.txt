45. ML Data Engineer 
Job Summary 
The ML Data Engineer builds and manages large-scale data pipelines that feed machine 
learning models with clean, reliable, and feature-rich datasets. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Develop and maintain data pipelines for ML workflows 
• Transform raw data into model-ready features 
• Collaborate with data scientists to support ML experiments 
• Ensure data quality, consistency, and reproducibility 
• Optimize pipelines for performance and scalability 
• Monitor data workflows and troubleshoot issues 
Required Skills 
• Strong programming in Python, Java, or Scala 
• Experience with Spark, Hadoop, or cloud-based data pipelines 
• Knowledge of SQL and data modeling 
• Familiarity with ML frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch, scikit-learn) 
• Understanding of feature engineering best practices 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or related field 
• 3–6 years of experience in ML data pipelines or big data engineering
