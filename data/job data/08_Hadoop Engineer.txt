8. Hadoop Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Hadoop Engineer focuses on building, maintaining, and optimizing Hadoop clusters and 
ensuring high availability, scalability, and performance of distributed data systems. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Install, configure, and manage Hadoop clusters 
• Monitor cluster health and performance 
• Optimize resource utilization and job scheduling 
• Implement security, backup, and recovery strategies 
• Troubleshoot cluster and performance issues 
• Work closely with DevOps and infrastructure teams 
Required Skills 
• Deep understanding of HDFS, YARN, and MapReduce 
• Experience with cluster management tools (Ambari, Cloudera, Hortonworks) 
• Strong Linux administration skills 
• Knowledge of Kerberos and Hadoop security 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering or equivalent 
• 3–6 years of Hadoop infrastructure experience
