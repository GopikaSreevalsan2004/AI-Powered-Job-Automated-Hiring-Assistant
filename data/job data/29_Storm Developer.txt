29. Storm Developer 
Job Summary 
The Storm Developer designs, implements, and maintains Apache Storm topologies for 
processing real-time streaming data efficiently and reliably. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Develop Storm topologies for data ingestion, processing, and analytics 
• Optimize performance, resource utilization, and fault tolerance 
• Integrate Storm with Kafka, HDFS, or databases 
• Monitor topologies and troubleshoot issues 
• Collaborate with data engineers and analysts 
Required Skills 
• Strong experience with Apache Storm 
• Programming in Java or Python 
• Understanding of distributed and parallel processing 
• Knowledge of real-time data processing patterns 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s degree 
• 3–5 years of experience with Storm or similar frameworks
