76. AI-Powered Data Engineer 
Job Summary 
The AI-Powered Data Engineer leverages generative AI and machine learning to optimize 
data pipelines, perform predictive transformations, and enhance analytics capabilities. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Build intelligent data pipelines with AI-assisted transformations 
• Implement predictive data quality and anomaly detection systems 
• Integrate AI services into ETL/ELT workflows 
• Collaborate with ML and analytics teams to enhance data-driven insights 
• Optimize AI-assisted data workflows for performance and cost 
Required Skills 
• Knowledge of generative AI tools and ML pipelines 
• Programming in Python, Java, or Scala 
• Experience with Spark, Hadoop, or cloud data platforms 
• Strong understanding of AI/ML lifecycle integration with data systems 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 4–8 years of experience in data engineering and AI integration
