65. Big Data Consultant 
Job Summary 
The Big Data Consultant provides expert guidance to organizations on designing, 
implementing, and optimizing big data strategies and platforms. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Assess client data architecture and provide recommendations 
• Design scalable and cost-effective big data solutions 
• Guide technology selection and implementation best practices 
• Advise on cloud migration, platform upgrades, and analytics enablement 
• Deliver workshops, documentation, and executive recommendations 
Required Skills 
• Deep knowledge of Hadoop, Spark, Kafka, Flink, and cloud platforms 
• Strong communication and client management skills 
• Experience with data governance, security, and compliance 
• Ability to translate business requirements into technical solutions 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 5–12 years of experience in big data architecture, consulting, or engineering 
• Proven client-facing consultancy experience
