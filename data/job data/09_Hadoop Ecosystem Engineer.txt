9. Hadoop Ecosystem Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Hadoop Ecosystem Engineer specializes in integrating and managing multiple Hadoop 
ecosystem components to deliver end-to-end big data solutions. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design solutions using Hive, HBase, Sqoop, Flume, Kafka, and Spark 
• Integrate Hadoop with relational and NoSQL databases 
• Develop data ingestion and transformation pipelines 
• Optimize performance across ecosystem components 
• Ensure data consistency and reliability 
• Support analytics and reporting use cases 
Required Skills 
• Strong experience across the Hadoop ecosystem 
• Programming in Java, Python, or Scala 
• Knowledge of batch and streaming data processing 
• Experience with data lakes 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in a relevant field 
• 4–7 years of Hadoop ecosystem experience
