75. Autonomous Data Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Autonomous Data Engineer designs self-managing data systems that minimize manual 
intervention using automation, AI, and orchestration tools. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Develop AI-driven data pipelines and autonomous workflows 
• Automate data quality checks, transformation, and error handling 
• Implement self-optimizing data ingestion and processing systems 
• Monitor and fine-tune pipelines with minimal human intervention 
• Integrate with ML and real-time analytics platforms 
Required Skills 
• Knowledge of MLOps, AI-driven orchestration, and workflow automation 
• Experience with Spark, Flink, Kafka, and distributed systems 
• Strong programming in Python, Scala, or Java 
• Cloud platform experience with serverless or autonomous services 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 5+ years of experience in big data and automated data engineering
