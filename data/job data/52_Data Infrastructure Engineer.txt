52. Data Infrastructure Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Data Infrastructure Engineer designs, deploys, and maintains the core infrastructure that 
powers large-scale data platforms and pipelines. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Architect and maintain scalable and reliable data infrastructure 
• Manage distributed storage, compute clusters, and data lakes 
• Implement automation for infrastructure provisioning and monitoring 
• Ensure high availability, fault tolerance, and disaster recovery 
• Collaborate with platform and data engineering teams 
Required Skills 
• Strong Linux/Unix administration skills 
• Experience with Hadoop, Spark, Kafka, and distributed storage 
• Cloud infrastructure expertise (AWS, Azure, GCP) 
• Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, CloudFormation, Ansible) 
• Monitoring and logging tools (Prometheus, Grafana, ELK Stack) 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 4–8 years of experience in data infrastructure or cloud operations
