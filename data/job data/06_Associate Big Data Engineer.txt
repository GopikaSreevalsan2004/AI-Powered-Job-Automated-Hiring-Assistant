️. Associate Big Data Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Associate Big Data Engineer works on entry-level data engineering tasks, supporting 
data processing, transformation, and system monitoring activities. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Support development of data workflows and pipelines 
• Assist in data ingestion and transformation tasks 
• Monitor data jobs and report issues 
• Document data processes and systems 
• Work closely with senior engineers to gain hands-on experience 
Required Skills 
• Understanding of data structures and databases 
• Basic programming and SQL skills 
• Familiarity with big data concepts 
• Good analytical and problem-solving skills 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s degree or diploma in Computer Science or related field 
• Internship or academic project experience in data engineering
