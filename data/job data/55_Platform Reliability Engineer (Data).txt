55. Platform Reliability Engineer (Data) 
Job Summary 
The Platform Reliability Engineer focuses on ensuring the performance, scalability, and 
reliability of enterprise-wide data platforms and pipelines. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and maintain reliable, scalable, and fault-tolerant data platforms 
• Implement platform monitoring, alerting, and automated remediation 
• Optimize compute, storage, and workflow performance 
• Collaborate with DevOps, data engineering, and ML teams 
• Support disaster recovery, backup, and high-availability strategies 
Required Skills 
• Strong knowledge of distributed data systems (Hadoop, Spark, Kafka) 
• Cloud and platform architecture expertise (AWS, GCP, Azure) 
• CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure automation 
• Observability and monitoring tools 
• Programming/scripting (Python, Bash, Go) 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 5–10 years of experience in platform reliability or data infrastructure
