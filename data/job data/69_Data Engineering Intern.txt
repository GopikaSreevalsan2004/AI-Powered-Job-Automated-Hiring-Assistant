69. Data Engineering Intern 
Job Summary 
The Data Engineering Intern works with mentors to gain practical experience in building and 
maintaining data pipelines, databases, and analytics platforms. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Assist in designing and implementing data ingestion and processing workflows 
• Support data cleaning, transformation, and integration tasks 
• Learn to work with cloud data platforms and big data frameworks 
• Contribute to documentation and testing of pipelines 
• Participate in team meetings and technical discussions 
Required Skills 
• Programming basics (Python, SQL, or Java) 
• Understanding of data structures and algorithms 
• Eagerness to learn big data tools and cloud platforms 
• Good communication and teamwork skills 
Qualifications 
• Undergraduate or graduate student in Computer Science, Data Science, or related 
field 
• Strong academic performance and interest in data engineering
