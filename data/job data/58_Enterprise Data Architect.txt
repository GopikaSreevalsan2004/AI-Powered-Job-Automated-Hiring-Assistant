58. Enterprise Data Architect 
Job Summary 
The Enterprise Data Architect designs the organization-wide data strategy, ensuring 
integration, quality, and governance of data assets across all systems. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Define enterprise data architecture, standards, and best practices 
• Align data strategy with business goals and analytics needs 
• Oversee integration across transactional, analytical, and big data systems 
• Ensure compliance, security, and regulatory adherence 
• Guide teams on data architecture design and implementation 
Required Skills 
• Enterprise-level data architecture and governance 
• Cloud, big data, and traditional data warehousing expertise 
• Strong communication and stakeholder management 
• SQL, ETL/ELT, and programming skills 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 10+ years of experience in data architecture across multiple systems
