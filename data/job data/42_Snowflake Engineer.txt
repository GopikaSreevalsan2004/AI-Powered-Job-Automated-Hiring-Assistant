42. Snowflake Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Snowflake Engineer specializes in designing and optimizing data pipelines and storage 
for Snowflake-based data warehouse solutions. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Develop and optimize ETL/ELT pipelines for Snowflake 
• Build and maintain data models and schemas 
• Optimize query performance and storage costs 
• Integrate Snowflake with analytics and BI tools 
• Ensure data security, compliance, and governance 
Required Skills 
• Expert in Snowflake architecture and features 
• Strong SQL and data modeling skills 
• Experience with Python or ETL/ELT tools 
• Knowledge of cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP) 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s degree 
• 3–6 years of experience with Snowflake
