36. Lakehouse Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Lakehouse Engineer designs, implements, and maintains modern data lakehouse 
architectures that combine the features of data lakes and data warehouses. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Implement lakehouse platforms using Delta Lake, Apache Hudi, or Iceberg 
• Build ETL/ELT pipelines for batch and streaming data 
• Optimize storage, query performance, and scalability 
• Ensure data governance, security, and consistency 
• Support analytics, BI, and ML workloads 
Required Skills 
• Expertise in lakehouse frameworks (Delta Lake, Hudi, Iceberg) 
• Strong SQL, Python, or Scala programming 
• Knowledge of Spark, Flink, or other distributed engines 
• Cloud experience with AWS, Azure, or GCP 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 4–7 years of experience in modern big data architectures
