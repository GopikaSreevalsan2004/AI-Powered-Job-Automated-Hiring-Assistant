5
️. Junior Big Data Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Junior Big Data Engineer supports the development and maintenance of data pipelines 
and big data platforms under the guidance of senior engineers. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Assist in building and maintaining data pipelines 
• Write and maintain ETL scripts 
• Perform data validation and quality checks 
• Support troubleshooting and issue resolution 
• Learn and apply best practices in big data engineering 
• Collaborate with team members on data projects 
Required Skills 
• Basic knowledge of Python, Java, or Scala 
• Familiarity with SQL and data concepts 
• Exposure to Hadoop or Spark is a plus 
• Strong willingness to learn 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s degree or final-year student in a relevant field 
• 0–2 years of experience
