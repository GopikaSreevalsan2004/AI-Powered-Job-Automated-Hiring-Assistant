54. Data SRE (Site Reliability Engineer) 
Job Summary 
The Data Site Reliability Engineer (SRE) applies software engineering principles to build 
highly reliable, scalable, and automated data platforms and services. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and implement highly reliable and scalable data systems 
• Build automation for deployment, scaling, and monitoring 
• Ensure SLA/SLO compliance for data pipelines and services 
• Troubleshoot complex operational issues and system failures 
• Collaborate with data engineers and platform teams to improve reliability 
Required Skills 
• Expertise in distributed systems and cloud platforms 
• Programming and automation (Python, Go, Java) 
• CI/CD and container orchestration (Kubernetes, Docker) 
• Monitoring, alerting, and observability (Prometheus, Grafana, ELK, Datadog) 
• Incident management and post-mortem analysis 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 5–8 years of experience in SRE or reliability engineering for data systems
