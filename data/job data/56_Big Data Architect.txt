56. Big Data Architect 
Job Summary 
The Big Data Architect designs and implements enterprise-scale big data solutions, ensuring 
efficient data storage, processing, and analytics across distributed systems. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design architecture for big data platforms, pipelines, and lakes 
• Evaluate and select big data technologies (Hadoop, Spark, Kafka, Flink) 
• Optimize data workflows for scalability, performance, and reliability 
• Ensure integration of structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data 
• Collaborate with engineering, analytics, and business teams 
• Define data security, governance, and compliance standards 
Required Skills 
• Deep expertise in big data frameworks and distributed systems 
• Strong programming and data modeling skills 
• Cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP) and data lakes/warehouses 
• System design, scalability, and performance optimization 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field 
• 8–12 years of experience in big data engineering and architecture
