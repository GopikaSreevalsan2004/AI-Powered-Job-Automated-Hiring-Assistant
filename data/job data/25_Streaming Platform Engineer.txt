25. Streaming Platform Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Streaming Platform Engineer builds, manages, and optimizes event-driven streaming 
platforms to support real-time analytics and applications. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and deploy streaming data platforms 
• Integrate multiple streaming technologies and data sources 
• Ensure scalability, reliability, and fault tolerance 
• Maintain platform security and compliance 
• Optimize cluster resources and streaming performance 
• Collaborate with data engineers, analysts, and DevOps teams 
Required Skills 
• Expertise in Kafka, Flink, or Spark Streaming 
• Knowledge of cloud-based streaming services (AWS Kinesis, Azure Event Hubs, 
GCP Pub/Sub) 
• Linux and cluster management skills 
• Familiarity with monitoring and alerting tools 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 4–7 years of experience in streaming platform engineering
