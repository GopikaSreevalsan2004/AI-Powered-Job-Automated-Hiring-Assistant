13. MapReduce Developer 
Job Summary 
The MapReduce Developer specializes in writing and optimizing MapReduce programs to 
process massive datasets efficiently within Hadoop environments. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and implement MapReduce jobs 
• Optimize data processing workflows 
• Debug and improve job performance 
• Manage data transformations at scale 
• Work closely with Hadoop engineers and analysts 
Required Skills 
• Strong Java programming skills 
• In-depth understanding of MapReduce framework 
• Knowledge of HDFS and YARN 
• Experience with large-scale batch processing 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or related field 
• 2–5 years of MapReduce development experience
