61. Director of Data Engineering 
Job Summary 
The Director of Data Engineering leads enterprise-wide data engineering operations, strategy, 
and architecture, driving innovation and operational excellence. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Define and execute data engineering strategy and roadmap 
• Oversee design, implementation, and maintenance of large-scale data platforms 
• Ensure cross-team collaboration and alignment with business objectives 
• Manage budgets, resources, and hiring for data engineering teams 
• Guide adoption of emerging technologies and best practices 
Required Skills 
• Leadership of large data engineering teams 
• Enterprise data architecture and big data platforms 
• Cloud platforms, distributed systems, and analytics 
• Strategic thinking, planning, and stakeholder communication 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 12–18 years of experience in data engineering and leadership
