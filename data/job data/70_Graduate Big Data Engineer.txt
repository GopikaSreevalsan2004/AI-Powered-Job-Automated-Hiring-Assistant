70. Graduate Big Data Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Graduate Big Data Engineer is an entry-level professional responsible for assisting in the 
design, development, and maintenance of big data solutions under guidance. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Build, test, and maintain data pipelines and ETL workflows 
• Work with distributed computing frameworks like Hadoop and Spark 
• Assist in integrating and transforming large datasets for analytics 
• Support troubleshooting, performance tuning, and data quality checks 
• Collaborate with senior engineers and data architects 
Required Skills 
• Programming in Python, Java, or Scala 
• SQL and relational database knowledge 
• Understanding of big data concepts and distributed systems 
• Willingness to learn cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP) 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or related field 
• Internship or project experience in data or analytics is preferred
