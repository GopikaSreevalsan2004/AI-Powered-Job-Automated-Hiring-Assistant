26. Kafka Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Kafka Engineer specializes in designing, implementing, and managing Apache Kafka 
clusters and related messaging systems for high-volume data streaming. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design, deploy, and maintain Kafka clusters 
• Implement topics, partitions, and replication strategies 
• Monitor and optimize Kafka performance and throughput 
• Ensure high availability and fault tolerance 
• Integrate Kafka with data pipelines and applications 
• Provide guidance on Kafka best practices 
Required Skills 
• Deep knowledge of Apache Kafka internals 
• Experience with Kafka Connect, Kafka Streams, and KSQL 
• Linux and scripting skills 
• Understanding of distributed systems and event-driven architecture 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or related field 
• 3–6 years of Kafka and streaming experience
