53. Data Reliability Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Data Reliability Engineer ensures the availability, stability, and performance of large
scale data systems and pipelines across the organization. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Monitor and maintain operational health of data pipelines and platforms 
• Implement alerts, monitoring, and automated remediation for data workflows 
• Identify and resolve performance bottlenecks and system failures 
• Collaborate with engineering, data science, and platform teams 
• Enforce best practices for reliability, scalability, and observability 
Required Skills 
• Strong experience in monitoring and observability tools (Prometheus, Grafana, ELK, 
Datadog) 
• Scripting and automation (Python, Bash) 
• Distributed data systems experience (Hadoop, Spark, Kafka) 
• Cloud experience (AWS, GCP, Azure) 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s degree 
• 4–7 years of experience in reliability engineering for data systems
