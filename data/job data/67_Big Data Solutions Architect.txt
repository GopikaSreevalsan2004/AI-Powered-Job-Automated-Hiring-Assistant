67. Big Data Solutions Architect 
Job Summary 
The Big Data Solutions Architect designs end-to-end big data platforms and solutions for 
clients, ensuring performance, scalability, and alignment with business goals. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Architect enterprise or startup big data solutions (cloud or on-premise) 
• Define technology stack, storage, and processing frameworks 
• Ensure scalability, fault tolerance, and security compliance 
• Collaborate with client engineering teams to deliver high-quality solutions 
• Provide technical leadership and documentation for deployed systems 
Required Skills 
• Deep expertise in big data ecosystems (Hadoop, Spark, Kafka, Flink) 
• Cloud architecture skills (AWS, Azure, GCP) 
• Strong system design, data modeling, and SQL skills 
• Excellent communication and stakeholder management abilities 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 8–15 years of experience in big data engineering and architecture 
• Proven track record of delivering enterprise-scale solutions
