49. Data Engineer – AI Platforms 
Job Summary 
The Data Engineer – AI Platforms builds and maintains the core data infrastructure for AI 
platforms, enabling efficient access, processing, and management of AI datasets. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and implement data pipelines for AI platform services 
• Ensure high-performance data storage and retrieval for AI workloads 
• Integrate structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data sources 
• Support AI feature engineering and model training workflows 
• Monitor data systems and ensure reliability and scalability 
Required Skills 
• Expertise in Spark, Hadoop, or cloud-native data platforms 
• Python or Scala programming skills 
• Knowledge of AI frameworks and large-scale ML datasets 
• Familiarity with data governance, lineage, and security 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 4–8 years of experience in AI or ML-focused data engineering
