12. Distributed Systems Engineer (Data) 
Job Summary 
The Distributed Systems Engineer (Data) designs and builds scalable, fault-tolerant 
distributed data systems that power large-scale analytics and data processing platforms. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and implement distributed data architectures 
• Ensure system scalability, fault tolerance, and consistency 
• Optimize data storage and processing frameworks 
• Address latency, throughput, and reliability challenges 
• Collaborate across engineering and infrastructure teams 
• Evaluate new distributed data technologies 
Required Skills 
• Strong understanding of distributed systems principles 
• Experience with Hadoop, Spark, Kafka, or similar systems 
• Proficiency in Java, Scala, or Go 
• Knowledge of CAP theorem, consensus, and replication 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 6–10 years of distributed systems experience
