62. VP of Data Engineering 
Job Summary 
The VP of Data Engineering is an executive leader responsible for the organization’s data 
engineering vision, strategy, and operations at a global or enterprise level. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Set the overall strategy and vision for data engineering 
• Oversee multiple data engineering divisions and enterprise platforms 
• Align data initiatives with organizational objectives and business strategy 
• Manage executive stakeholders and drive innovation in data capabilities 
• Ensure operational excellence, scalability, and security of all data systems 
Required Skills 
• Executive leadership and strategic planning 
• Deep expertise in big data, cloud platforms, analytics, and enterprise data architecture 
• Financial management, resource planning, and organizational leadership 
• Strong communication, negotiation, and stakeholder engagement 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 15+ years of experience in data engineering, architecture, and leadership
