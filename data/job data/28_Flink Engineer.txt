28. Flink Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Flink Engineer builds high-performance, scalable, and low-latency data pipelines using 
Apache Flink for real-time analytics and operational intelligence. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Develop and maintain Flink applications 
• Optimize Flink jobs for performance and fault tolerance 
• Implement stateful streaming pipelines 
• Integrate Flink with Kafka, HDFS, or cloud storage 
• Monitor, debug, and tune streaming workloads 
Required Skills 
• Strong expertise in Apache Flink 
• Programming in Java or Scala 
• Knowledge of distributed stream processing concepts 
• Experience with real-time analytics 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or related field 
• 3–6 years of Flink or stream processing experience
