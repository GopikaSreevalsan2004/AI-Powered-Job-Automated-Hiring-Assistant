72. Apprentice Big Data Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Apprentice Big Data Engineer learns foundational skills in big data engineering while 
contributing to small-scale data projects and assisting the engineering team. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Support development of data pipelines and ETL processes 
• Assist in data extraction, cleaning, and transformation 
• Learn to use big data tools and cloud platforms under guidance 
• Participate in training sessions, workshops, and team discussions 
• Document workflows and follow standard engineering practices 
Required Skills 
• Basic knowledge of Python, SQL, or Java 
• Familiarity with relational databases and data structures 
• Eagerness to learn big data technologies and analytics 
• Good communication and collaboration skills 
Qualifications 
• Recent graduate, undergraduate, or final-year student in a relevant field 
• Willingness to undergo hands-on training and mentorship
