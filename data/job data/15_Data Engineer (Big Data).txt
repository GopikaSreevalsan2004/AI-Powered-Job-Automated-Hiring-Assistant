5. Data Engineer (Big Data) 
Job Summary 
The Data Engineer (Big Data) designs, builds, and maintains scalable data pipelines and 
platforms that process large volumes of data for analytics, reporting, and machine learning. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and implement big data pipelines using Spark, Hadoop, and SQL 
• Build and maintain ETL/ELT workflows for structured and unstructured data 
• Integrate data from multiple sources including APIs, databases, and logs 
• Ensure data quality, reliability, and performance 
• Optimize storage and query performance 
• Collaborate with data scientists, analysts, and product teams 
Required Skills 
• Programming in Python, Java, or Scala 
• Strong SQL and data modeling skills 
• Experience with Hadoop, Spark, Hive 
• Familiarity with cloud data platforms 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or related field 
• 3–5 years of data engineering experience
