66. Data Engineering Consultant 
Job Summary 
The Data Engineering Consultant helps organizations implement, optimize, and scale data 
pipelines and engineering practices for analytics and ML initiatives. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and implement data pipelines and ETL/ELT workflows 
• Optimize data storage, processing, and workflow performance 
• Mentor and guide client teams on best practices and standards 
• Support cloud adoption, data governance, and platform modernization 
• Provide actionable insights and technical recommendations 
Required Skills 
• Expertise in Spark, Hadoop, Kafka, SQL, and cloud data platforms 
• Strong Python, Scala, or Java programming skills 
• Familiarity with data modeling, warehousing, and analytics platforms 
• Excellent client-facing and advisory capabilities 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 5–10 years of experience in data engineering and consulting 
• Previous experience in client-facing data projects
