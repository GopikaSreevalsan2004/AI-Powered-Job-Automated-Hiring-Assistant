77. Real-Time Analytics Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Real-Time Analytics Engineer designs and maintains low-latency, streaming data 
systems for instant insights, monitoring, and analytics. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Build streaming pipelines using Kafka, Kinesis, Flink, or Storm 
• Implement real-time data ingestion, transformation, and aggregation 
• Optimize pipelines for latency, throughput, and reliability 
• Support dashboards, anomaly detection, and AI/ML real-time models 
• Monitor and troubleshoot production streaming systems 
Required Skills 
• Expertise in real-time streaming platforms (Kafka, Flink, Kinesis, Storm) 
• Programming in Python, Java, or Scala 
• SQL and distributed systems knowledge 
• Cloud experience for serverless or real-time processing 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 4–7 years of experience in streaming and real-time data engineering
