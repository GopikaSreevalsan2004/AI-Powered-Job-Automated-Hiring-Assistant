79. Privacy-Preserving Data Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Privacy-Preserving Data Engineer builds data pipelines and platforms that maintain 
compliance, anonymity, and confidentiality using advanced privacy techniques. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Implement data anonymization, encryption, and differential privacy techniques 
• Ensure compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and other regulations 
• Design secure pipelines for analytics, ML, and AI workflows 
• Collaborate with legal, compliance, and engineering teams 
• Monitor and audit privacy-preserving measures in data systems 
Required Skills 
• Knowledge of privacy-preserving technologies (differential privacy, federated 
learning, homomorphic encryption) 
• Strong programming in Python, Scala, or Java 
• SQL and data engineering expertise 
• Cloud and distributed data platform experience 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 4–8 years of experience in secure data engineering and compliance
