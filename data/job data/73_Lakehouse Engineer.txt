73. Lakehouse Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Lakehouse Engineer designs and manages modern data lakehouse architectures, 
combining the scalability of data lakes with the performance and governance of data 
warehouses. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Build and maintain unified data lakehouse platforms (Delta Lake, Apache Hudi, 
Apache Iceberg) 
• Develop ETL/ELT pipelines for batch and streaming workloads 
• Optimize storage, query performance, and cost efficiency 
• Implement governance, access controls, and data lineage 
• Support analytics, BI, and AI/ML workloads 
Required Skills 
• Expertise in lakehouse frameworks (Delta Lake, Hudi, Iceberg) 
• Spark, Flink, or other distributed computing experience 
• SQL, Python, or Scala programming 
• Cloud platform experience (AWS, Azure, GCP) 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Data Engineering, or related 
field 
• 4–7 years of experience in modern big data architectures
