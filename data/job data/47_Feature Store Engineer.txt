47. Feature Store Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Feature Store Engineer designs, builds, and maintains feature stores that provide 
consistent, reusable features for machine learning models. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Develop and maintain centralized feature stores 
• Ensure features are accessible, versioned, and reproducible 
• Automate feature extraction, transformation, and validation 
• Collaborate with data scientists and ML engineers 
• Monitor feature usage and performance 
Required Skills 
• Experience with feature store platforms (Feast, Tecton, Hopsworks) 
• Strong programming in Python or Scala 
• Knowledge of Spark, Hadoop, or cloud pipelines 
• Understanding of ML feature engineering and modeling 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 3–6 years of experience in ML or data engineering
