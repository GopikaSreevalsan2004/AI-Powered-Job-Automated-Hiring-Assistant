23. Data Platform Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Data Platform Engineer designs and maintains enterprise data platforms that support 
analytics, machine learning, and business intelligence at scale. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Build and manage scalable data platforms and frameworks 
• Develop reusable data services and tools 
• Ensure platform reliability, security, and scalability 
• Support multiple data consumers across the organization 
• Drive platform modernization initiatives 
Required Skills 
• Strong knowledge of big data and cloud platforms 
• Experience with data lakes, lakehouses, and warehouses 
• Platform engineering and DevOps practices 
• Automation and monitoring tools 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 
• 6–10 years of data platform engineering experience
