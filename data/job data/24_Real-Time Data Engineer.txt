24. Real-Time Data Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Real-Time Data Engineer designs and implements high-throughput, low-latency data 
pipelines to process streaming and event-driven data for analytics and operational systems. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Develop and maintain real-time data pipelines 
• Implement streaming architectures using Kafka, Flink, Spark Streaming, or Storm 
• Ensure data consistency, reliability, and low-latency delivery 
• Collaborate with data scientists and analysts for real-time analytics 
• Monitor and optimize streaming workloads and infrastructure 
• Troubleshoot and resolve streaming system issues 
Required Skills 
• Programming in Java, Scala, or Python 
• Strong knowledge of Kafka, Spark Streaming, Flink, or Storm 
• Experience with distributed systems and event-driven architectures 
• SQL and real-time data transformation expertise 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field 
• 3–6 years of real-time data engineering experience
