38. Analytics Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Analytics Engineer bridges the gap between data engineering and business analytics, 
building reliable and reusable data models and pipelines for reporting and decision-making. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Develop and maintain analytics-focused data pipelines 
• Build data models to support dashboards, reporting, and BI 
• Collaborate with analysts, product teams, and data scientists 
• Optimize data transformations for performance and scalability 
• Ensure data quality, consistency, and lineage 
• Implement version control and CI/CD for analytics code 
Required Skills 
• SQL expertise and data modeling experience 
• Experience with ETL/ELT tools and workflow orchestration (Airflow, dbt) 
• Python or R for analytics pipelines 
• Familiarity with cloud data warehouses and analytics platforms 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or related field 
• 2–5 years of experience in analytics engineering or data modeling
