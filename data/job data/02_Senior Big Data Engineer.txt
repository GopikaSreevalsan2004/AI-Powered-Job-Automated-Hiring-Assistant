2. Senior Big Data Engineer 
Job Summary 
The Senior Big Data Engineer leads complex data engineering initiatives, designs high
performance data architectures, and mentors junior engineers while ensuring system 
scalability and reliability. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and implement enterprise-scale big data architectures 
• Optimize performance of data pipelines and storage systems 
• Lead complex data integration and migration projects 
• Review code and enforce engineering best practices 
• Mentor junior and mid-level engineers 
• Collaborate with stakeholders on data strategy and requirements 
• Ensure security, compliance, and governance standards 
Required Skills 
• Advanced Spark optimization and tuning 
• Strong experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP) 
• Deep knowledge of data lakes and data warehouses 
• Strong problem-solving and architectural skills 
Qualifications 
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in a relevant field 
• 5–8 years of experience in Big Data Engineering
