68. Big Data Engineer Intern 
Job Summary 
The Big Data Engineer Intern assists in developing and maintaining big data pipelines and 
platforms while gaining hands-on experience with distributed systems and cloud 
technologies. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Assist in building and maintaining data pipelines and ETL/ELT workflows 
• Work with structured and unstructured data sources 
• Support testing, debugging, and documentation of data solutions 
• Learn and apply big data technologies like Hadoop, Spark, and Kafka 
• Collaborate with senior engineers on data platform projects 
Required Skills 
• Basic programming skills (Python, Java, or Scala) 
• Understanding of SQL and relational databases 
• Interest in distributed systems, cloud platforms, and big data 
• Analytical thinking and problem-solving skills 
Qualifications 
• Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Data 
Science, or related field 
• No prior work experience required; internships or projects are a plus
